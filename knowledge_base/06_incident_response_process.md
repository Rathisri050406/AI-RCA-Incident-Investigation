# Incident Response Process (project summary of common guidance)
Widely used guidance such as NIST SP 800-61 (Computer Security Incident Handling Guide) organises response into preparation, detection and analysis, containment, eradication and recovery, and post-incident activity.
Good practice: record evidence and timeline, separate facts from inferences, assess impact and severity, and keep a human decision-maker in charge of disruptive containment such as blocking addresses or disabling accounts.
Every incident should end with a report covering what happened, evidence, root cause, confidence, impact, response taken and lessons learned.
